## JTBDs
1. Discover talks that match their interests by track, date, and format
2. Know exactly when and where a talk takes place and whether it is still on
3. Understand what a talk covers and who delivers it
4. Learn who a speaker is and which other talks they present
5. Know which tracks the conference offers
6. Find a specific speaker by name
