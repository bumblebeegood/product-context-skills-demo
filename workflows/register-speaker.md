# Register Speaker

## Context
Organizer adding a presenter.

## Role
Conference Organizer

## Prerequisites
- The organizer is authenticated (BR-ACC-02).

## Steps
1. Organizer provides name, title, and optionally company, bio, photo URL, and website.
2. System validates the fields against the Speaker data example.
3. System saves the speaker; the speaker appears in the alphabetical list and in name search immediately (BR-SPK-02, BR-SYN-03).

## Expected Result
A registered speaker who can be assigned to talks.
