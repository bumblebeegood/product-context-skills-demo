# Find My Talk Room and Time Slot

## Context
Speaker locating their own session. No speaker login exists, so the speaker searches by name.

## Role
Speaker

## Prerequisites
- The speaker is registered and has at least one talk.

## Steps
1. Speaker searches for their own name (see `find-speaker-by-name.md`).
2. System returns matching speakers alphabetically (BR-SPK-02, BR-SPK-03); speaker opens their own profile.
3. System shows their talks ordered by start time with room, start and end time, and status (BR-PRG-05).
4. Speaker opens a talk to see the other speakers and the track (BR-PRG-03).

## Expected Result
The speaker knows the room and time slot of each of their talks and who else is on their track.
